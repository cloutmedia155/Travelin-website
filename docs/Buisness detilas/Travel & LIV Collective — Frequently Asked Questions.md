# **Travel & LIV Collective — Frequently Asked Questions**

Everything you need to know before you book, pack, and meet your new travel crew.

Travel & LIV Collective creates curated group trips for people who want more than a vacation. We combine unforgettable destinations, 4–5-star stays, thoughtfully planned experiences, and intentional opportunities to connect. Come solo, come with a friend, or come with your partner—either way, you will not have to plan the trip alone.

## **About Travel & LIV**

### **What is Travel & LIV Collective?**

Travel & LIV Collective is a community-centered group travel company. We create curated international trips that bring together people who value new experiences, meaningful connections, quality accommodations, and a good time.

This is not a large, impersonal tour group or a packed sightseeing schedule. Each trip is intentionally designed to balance exploring, connecting, celebrating, and actually enjoying the destination.

### **What makes Travel & LIV different from other group trips?**

We do more than put a hotel and a few excursions into a package. Every trip is built around both the destination and the people experiencing it.

Depending on the trip, that may include pre-trip meet-and-greets, welcome events, group dinners, social mixers, cultural experiences, adventure days, nightlife, and free time. Our goal is for you to return home with more than pictures—you should leave with real memories and new connections.

### **Who joins Travel & LIV trips?**

Our travelers are typically experience-driven adults in their Mid 20s, 30s, and early 40s. Many are professionals, entrepreneurs, creatives, and frequent or aspiring travelers who enjoy beautiful places, elevated experiences, great food, nightlife, and meeting new people.

You do not need to fit one specific personality type. We simply ask that travelers arrive open-minded, respectful, flexible, and ready to be part of the group.

### **Is there an age requirement?**

Most Travel & LIV trips are designed for adults ages 21 and older. Some experiences—especially singles trips or trips involving adults-only resorts—may have a more specific age range. Always review the individual trip page before booking.

### **Do I have to know someone to join?**

Not at all. Many of our travelers book solo. We create opportunities to meet before the trip and connect naturally once you arrive, so you will not feel like you are walking into a room where everyone already knows each other.

### **Can I join with a friend, partner, or family member?**

Yes, unless the trip is specifically described as a singles-only or adults-only experience. Friends, couples, siblings, and relatives are welcome on our general trips. Each traveler must complete their own registration and follow the same payment and travel requirements.

### **Are singles trips only for people looking for a relationship?**

No. Our singles trips are for unattached adults who are open to meeting new people. Some travelers may be interested in romance, while others may simply want to expand their social circle and travel with other singles.

We create the environment for genuine connections, but we never promise romantic matches or pressure anyone to date.

### **How large are the groups?**

Group size varies by destination, accommodations, and activity capacity. We intentionally limit each trip so the experience still feels personal and the group can genuinely connect. The individual trip page will note the expected size or available capacity when applicable.

### **Is Travel & LIV inclusive?**

Yes. We welcome travelers from different backgrounds, cultures, identities, and life experiences. Respect is non-negotiable. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, threatening behavior, or conduct that compromises another traveler’s safety or experience will not be tolerated.

## **The Trip Experience**

### **What does “mid-luxury” mean?**

For us, mid-luxury means thoughtfully selected 4–5-star hotels, resorts, villas, or boutique accommodations; curated experiences; reliable transportation for scheduled activities; and special touches that make the trip feel elevated.

It does not mean every meal, drink, upgrade, or personal expense is included. Always review the specific trip page for the exact inclusions.

### **What is included in my trip package?**

Inclusions vary by trip, but packages may include:

* Accommodations  
* Welcome and/or farewell events  
* Curated group activities and excursions  
* Transportation for scheduled group activities  
* Select meals and drinks  
* Airport transfers during designated arrival and departure windows  
* On-the-ground hosting and trip support  
* Access to the private trip group chat  
* A Travel & LIV goodie bag or welcome gift

The inclusions listed on the individual trip page and booking checkout are the ones that apply to your reservation.

### **What is not included?**

Unless a trip page specifically says otherwise, your package generally does not include:

* International airfare  
* Passport or visa fees  
* Travel insurance  
* Vaccinations, testing, or other health-related costs  
* Meals and drinks not listed in the itinerary  
* Optional excursions or nightlife  
* Personal shopping, spa services, tips, or room charges  
* Transportation outside scheduled group activities  
* Costs caused by flight disruptions, missed transfers, or personal itinerary changes

### **Will Travel & LIV help me choose a flight?**

Yes. Once the trip is confirmed, we will share recommended arrival and departure windows, airport details, and general flight guidance. You are responsible for booking and managing your own airfare.

Please do not book a nonrefundable flight until you receive written confirmation that the trip is confirmed and flight booking is recommended.

### **Are airport transfers included?**

Airport transfers are included only when listed on the trip page or itinerary. Transfers may be limited to a specific airport and designated arrival and departure windows.

If you arrive early, leave late, use a different airport, or miss the scheduled transfer because of a delay, you may be responsible for arranging and paying for your own transportation.

### **How busy are the itineraries?**

Our itineraries are active, but we do not want you to feel like you need a vacation after your vacation. Most trips include a mix of scheduled experiences, social events, cultural activities, and free time.

Some destinations require early mornings, walking, stairs, boat transfers, long drives, heat, or moderate physical activity. Review the itinerary carefully and contact us before booking if you have mobility, health, or accessibility concerns.

### **Do I have to attend every activity?**

No. This is your vacation, and you may opt out of an activity. However, missed activities, meals, transportation, or events are generally not refundable. If you leave the group itinerary, you are responsible for your own transportation, expenses, timing, and safety until you rejoin the group.

Please tell the host before separating from the group so we know you are safe and do not delay everyone else.

### **Is nightlife part of the experience?**

Many Travel & LIV trips include social evenings, lounges, beach clubs, bars, or nightlife because our community enjoys having fun after the daytime activities. Nightlife is optional unless it is specifically listed as an included group event.

Cover charges, food, drinks, tables, and transportation for optional outings may be at your own expense. Travelers are expected to drink responsibly and remain respectful of the group, local laws, and the host property.

### **Are meals included?**

Meal inclusions vary by trip. Some packages include daily breakfast and selected group meals; all-inclusive resort trips may include most meals and drinks. Your trip page will clearly identify what is included.

Any meal, beverage, room-service order, dietary upgrade, or restaurant visit not listed as included is your responsibility.

### **Can you accommodate dietary restrictions or allergies?**

We will communicate dietary restrictions and allergies to our vendors when possible, but we cannot guarantee that every hotel, restaurant, or destination can accommodate every request or prevent cross-contamination.

Please disclose serious allergies during registration and remind the host and food-service staff during the trip. Travelers with severe allergies should carry any necessary medication and have a personal safety plan.

### **Will the itinerary ever change?**

It can. International travel sometimes requires flexibility. Weather, safety concerns, vendor availability, local closures, transportation delays, group needs, or circumstances outside our control may require us to adjust the itinerary, timing, accommodations, or order of activities.

When changes are necessary, we will aim to provide a comparable experience whenever reasonably possible and communicate updates as quickly as we can.

### **Will photos and videos be taken during the trip?**

Yes. Travel & LIV trips are highly visual, and our host, team members, vendors, photographers, or fellow travelers may capture photos and videos throughout the experience. Content may be shared in the private trip album or featured on Travel & LIV’s website and social platforms, subject to the media terms accepted during booking.

We also encourage travelers to take pictures and videos of one another and upload their favorite moments to the shared album. If you have a specific privacy concern, contact us before the trip so we can explain the available options.

## **Booking, Payments & Cancellations**

### **How do I secure my spot?**

Choose your trip and room type, complete the booking form, accept the traveler terms, and submit the required deposit or full payment. Your place is not reserved by joining a waitlist, commenting on social media, entering a WhatsApp community, or telling us you plan to attend.

Your spot is secured only after the booking platform confirms your payment.

### **If I can still book online, is the trip still available?**

Generally, yes. Our booking pages close when a room type or trip sells out. Availability can change quickly, and a spot is not held until payment is successfully completed.

### **Do you offer payment plans?**

Yes. Most trips offer scheduled installment plans, and some checkout options may include third-party financing such as Affirm, Klarna, or Afterpay when available.

Payment schedules, eligibility, fees, and financing terms vary. Review the checkout details carefully before committing. Using a payment plan does not change the cancellation or refund policy.

### **What happens if I miss a payment?**

Contact us as soon as possible—preferably before the due date. Late or missed payments may result in a fee, loss of your reservation, or cancellation of your payment plan in accordance with the terms accepted at booking.

We cannot guarantee extensions because your payments are used to meet deadlines with hotels, transportation companies, and activity providers.

### **Are deposits refundable?**

Deposits are all payments made to Travel & LIV Colelctive are nonrefundable because they reserve limited inventory and are used toward planning and vendor commitments. Any transfer or credit exception must follow the written terms attached to your specific booking and is not guaranteed.

We strongly recommend purchasing travel insurance that may protect your nonrefundable costs for covered reasons.

### **What if I need to cancel?**

Notify Travel & LIV in writing as soon as possible. Refunds, credits, or transfers are determined by the cancellation policy and traveler agreement accepted when you booked. Because we make nonrefundable payments to vendors in advance, personal cancellations—including changes involving work, relationships, finances, illness, flight issues, or other personal circumstances—may not qualify for a refund.

Travel insurance is the best way to protect yourself against many unexpected situations, subject to the policy you purchase.

### **Can I transfer my reservation to another person or trip?**

Transfers are not automatic. Name changes and trip credits depend on timing, vendor rules, room availability, price differences, administrative costs, and the terms of your reservation. Contact us before promising or selling your spot to someone else.

### **What happens if Travel & LIV cancels or postpones a trip?**

If Travel & LIV cancels a trip, travelers will receive the options required by the applicable booking terms. If a trip must be postponed or materially changed, we will communicate the available next steps in writing.

Travel & LIV is not responsible for independently booked airfare, hotels, or other personal expenses. This is another reason we recommend waiting for confirmation before purchasing flights and choosing travel insurance with appropriate cancellation and interruption coverage.

### **What happens if the trip does not reach its minimum group size?**

Some trips require a minimum number of travelers. If the minimum is not reached, Travel & LIV may cancel, postpone, or modify the experience in accordance with the applicable booking terms. We will notify registered travelers and explain their available options.

### **Are prices guaranteed?**

The price shown at the time you complete your booking applies to the room and package you selected, subject to the booking terms. Promotional pricing, discounts, and coupon codes may expire and generally cannot be added retroactively.

Changes you request—such as upgrading to a private room, adding an excursion, or transferring to another trip—may require an additional payment.

## **Rooms & Roommate Matching**

### **What is the difference between single and double occupancy?**

Single occupancy means you have a private bedroom or hotel room. Double occupancy means two paying travelers share a room; depending on the property, the room may have two beds or one shared bed.

Always read the room description before booking. Bedding configurations are controlled by the hotel or property and may not be guaranteed unless specifically confirmed in writing.

### **Can I book double occupancy with someone I know?**

Yes. Both travelers should book the matching room category and list each other as requested roommates during registration. A room is not fully secured until both travelers have registered and paid the required amount.

### **Can I book double occupancy if I am traveling alone?**

Yes, when the trip offers solo roommate matching. We will pair you with another registered traveler, generally of the same gender, based on availability and the information provided in your roommate questionnaire.

We will make a thoughtful match, but we cannot guarantee that roommates will have identical habits, personalities, sleep schedules, or preferences.

### **When will I find out who my roommate is?**

Roommate assignments are usually shared closer to departure, after bookings and rooming lists are finalized. You will have an opportunity to connect before the trip through the private trip group chat.

### **What if my requested roommate cancels?**

If your roommate cancels or falls behind on payments, contact us promptly. Depending on availability, you may be assigned another roommate, asked to pay a single-occupancy supplement, or offered another available option under the applicable booking terms.

Travel & LIV cannot absorb the cost of an empty bed created by another traveler’s cancellation.

### **Can I change room types after booking?**

Maybe. Changes depend on availability, hotel rules, occupancy limits, and price differences. Submit your request in writing as early as possible. Your room type is not changed until Travel & LIV confirms it and any additional balance is paid.

### **Can I bring an unregistered guest to my room or a group activity?**

No, unless Travel & LIV and the hotel or activity provider approve it in advance. Accommodations, transportation, and activities are reserved for registered travelers, and many properties have strict occupancy and guest policies.

## **Preparing for Your Trip**

### **Do I need a passport or visa?**

For international trips, you are responsible for having a valid passport, any required visa or entry authorization, and enough passport validity and blank pages for the destination. Entry rules vary by citizenship and can change.

Travel & LIV may share general reminders, but we cannot determine your individual eligibility to enter a country. Confirm requirements with the relevant embassy, consulate, or official government source before booking flights.

### **Do I need travel insurance?**

We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance for every traveler, and some trips may require proof of coverage. Look for protection that fits your needs, including trip cancellation or interruption, medical care abroad, emergency evacuation, baggage issues, and travel delays.

Read the policy carefully. Coverage depends on the provider, the reason for the claim, purchase date, exclusions, and policy limits.

### **Are vaccinations or health documents required?**

Requirements depend on the destination, your travel history, and current government rules. You are responsible for checking entry requirements and consulting a qualified healthcare professional about recommended vaccinations, medications, and health precautions.

### **What travel documents will I receive?**

Before departure, registered travelers will receive the essential trip information, which may include the final itinerary, accommodation details, arrival instructions, packing guidance, dress themes, emergency contacts, and group expectations.

Some hotel or vendor details may be shared closer to departure for operational or security reasons.

### **When will I be added to the trip group chat?**

Individual trip chats are reserved for travelers who have officially booked. After your registration is confirmed, you will receive instructions for joining the private chat.

The general Travel & LIV WhatsApp community, social pages, and waitlists are open to people interested in future trips, but joining them does not reserve a place on a trip.

### **Will there be a pre-trip meet-and-greet?**

Many trips include a virtual or in-person meet-and-greet so travelers can meet the host, ask questions, review expectations, and start connecting before departure. Details will be shared with registered travelers when applicable.

### **What should I pack?**

We will share destination-specific guidance and any planned dress themes before departure. In general, pack for the weather, planned activities, local customs, and your personal needs. You are responsible for baggage fees, airline restrictions, medication, adapters, and any items not supplied by the trip.

## **Safety, Support & Group Expectations**

### **Is group travel with Travel & LIV safe?**

We carefully plan our experiences, work with established accommodations and local partners, provide trip support, and communicate important safety information. However, no destination or travel experience is risk-free, and Travel & LIV cannot guarantee your personal safety.

Travelers are expected to use good judgment, follow local laws and host instructions, monitor their belongings, drink responsibly, and avoid unsafe situations. If you choose to leave the group, you assume responsibility for your independent plans and transportation.

### **Will someone from Travel & LIV be present?**

Most hosted trips include a Travel & LIV host, co-host, or designated on-the-ground point of contact. The team manages the group itinerary and supports travelers with trip-related logistics.

Trip hosts are not personal assistants, medical professionals, security guards, or 24-hour concierge staff. Travelers remain responsible for their personal choices, documents, belongings, health, and conduct.

### **What happens in an emergency?**

If there is an immediate emergency, contact local emergency services first, then notify the Travel & LIV host as soon as it is safe to do so. The host can help communicate with local partners and assist with reasonable trip-related coordination, but any medical, legal, transportation, or personal expenses remain the traveler’s responsibility unless covered by insurance or stated otherwise.

### **What behavior is expected from travelers?**

We want every traveler to feel respected and able to enjoy the experience. Travelers must follow local laws, property rules, activity safety instructions, the agreed itinerary, and Travel & LIV’s code of conduct.

Repeated lateness, harassment, fighting, illegal activity, unsafe behavior, excessive disruption, or conduct that negatively affects the group may result in removal from an activity or trip without a refund. Any additional lodging, transportation, legal, or travel costs would be the traveler’s responsibility.

### **Why is punctuality important on a group trip?**

Tours, restaurant reservations, transportation, and attraction tickets run on fixed schedules. One late traveler can cause the entire group to miss an experience.

Please be ready before the stated departure time. If you miss scheduled transportation or an activity, you may need to arrange your own transportation at your expense, and the group may be unable to wait.

### **What if I need accessibility accommodations?**

Contact us before booking with specific details about your mobility, medical, sensory, or accessibility needs. We will ask our partners what can reasonably be accommodated, but accessibility standards vary widely by country, property, vehicle, and activity.

We cannot guarantee an accommodation unless it has been confirmed in writing.

### **Can I extend my stay before or after the group trip?**

Absolutely. Many travelers choose to arrive early or stay longer. Any extra flights, accommodations, transfers, meals, visas, and activities are separate from the Travel & LIV package unless we confirm otherwise in writing.

Make sure your personal plans do not interfere with the official arrival window or scheduled start of the trip.
